Who We Are

HARMONY PACK

Founded in Jiaxing, China, HARMONY PACK is a professional manufacturer focusing on eco-friendly food packaging products. Our main products include sugarcane pulp containers, sushi boxes, kraft paper bags, sauce bottles and other disposable food packaging for wholesale export.

We have complete certification: FSC, ISO9001, BRCGS, FDA. We provide customized packaging solutions for global buyers, covering Amazon and Alibaba International station customers.

We insist on environmental protection, stable quality and competitive price to build long-term cooperation with customers all over the world.
