Who We Are

20+ years dedicated to food packaging manufacturing

Jiaxing Harmony Packaging Products Co., Ltd. is a professional manufacturer specializing in food-grade packaging solutions, with over 20 years of industry experience. The company offers flexible production capabilities, leveraging its decade-long contract manufacturing expertise to deliver high-quality, customized packaging for global clients.

The company produces and sells all kinds of plastic (PP, PS, PET) and degradable materials (bagasse and kraft paper) food packaging container, for customers from product design, proofing, mold development, product manufacturing to provide satisfactory services.
